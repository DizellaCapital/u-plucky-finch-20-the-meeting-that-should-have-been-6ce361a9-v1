One line about a meeting that should have been an email — a [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry by [@dizellacapital](https://make.fun/r/qxacafna).

## The joke

> **At 9:03, Dave opened slide 47 of a 'quick sync,' and by slide 48 I had drafted his eulogy, my resignation, and the email this meeting should have been.**

At 9:03, Dave opens slide 47 of a quick sync — and by slide 48 I have drafted his eulogy, my resignation, and the email this meeting should have been.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the joke to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), validates it is a single line of 1–200 characters with no tabs and sentence-ending punctuation, and verifies the write by reading it back. The entry file is exactly 151 bytes, with no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat "$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt"
```
